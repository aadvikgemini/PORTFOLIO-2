LEGAL & TRANSPARENCY

PRIVACY POLICY.

This page describes what limited information may be processed when you browse this portfolio, submit the contact form, or choose to enable optional analytics.

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1. Information Collected

Browsing the static pages of this portfolio does not require creating an account or providing personal details. Personal information is collected only when you voluntarily submit the contact form, or when you explicitly opt in to optional analytics measurement.

2. Contact Form Submissions

When you submit the contact form on the homepage, the following fields are transmitted to the server endpoint solely to read and respond to your inquiry:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message subject and message content

Submitted contact messages are routed through the configured server function and transactional email delivery provider to Aadvik Gemini's inbox and are not sold or used for marketing lists.

3. Spam Protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

To protect the contact form against automated spam and abuse, this website integrates Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile validates verification tokens server-side and may inspect technical browser signals as part of Cloudflare's bot-mitigation process.

4. Google Analytics 4 & Cookies

Google Analytics 4 is configured to load only after you grant analytics consent via the preference banner (and only when a Measurement ID is configured). Your consent preference is stored locally in your browser via localStorage under the key ag_analytics_consent.

If you accept analytics and a GA4 Measurement ID is active, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to measure aggregate page views, navigation interactions, and contact form submission events. If you decline, Google Analytics scripts are not loaded.

5. Third-Party Services

Depending on deployment configuration, third-party infrastructure providers involved in delivering this website include:

  • Google Fonts (for loading the Anton and Onest typefaces)
  • jsDelivr CDN (for loading the Lenis smooth-scrolling library)
  • Cloudflare (for hosting, HTTPS, and Turnstile verification)
  • Configured transactional email provider for forwarding contact form inquiries
  • Google Analytics 4 (only if consented and configured)

6. Contact & Inquiries Regarding Your Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to ask about a message you submitted through the contact form, please reach out through the Contact section on the homepage.
